# Product Vision

Content Factory is a private, multi-user, multi-channel editorial operating system.

It is not "an app that makes AI videos."

It coordinates:
Discovery → Evidence → Truth Source → Content Idea → Script → Storyboard → Assets → Audio → Render → QA → Publication → Metrics → Learning.

## Product principles

1. Originality is transformative, not paraphrasing.
2. Every published item has reproducible lineage.
3. Human approval remains at high-leverage gates until evidence justifies more automation.
4. Automation grows from observed bottlenecks.
5. Channels are editorial identities, not social accounts.
6. Measure before scaling.
7. AI providers are replaceable configuration.
8. n8n orchestrates; it does not own domain state or product rules.
9. The dashboard is the control center from the first slice.
10. The product should become useful in weeks, not months.
