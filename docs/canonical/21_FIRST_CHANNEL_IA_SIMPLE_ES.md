# Initial Channel — IA Simple ES

Status: pilot

Language: Spanish  
Niche: AI and future of work

## Audience

Spanish-speaking adults interested in understanding practical implications of AI, tools and changes in work without hype.

## Promise

Explain relevant AI/work changes clearly and usefully in short form.

## Tone

- clear;
- sober;
- curious;
- practical;
- accessible without being childish;
- avoid sensational certainty.

## Initial format

Short vertical video:
- roughly 45–60 seconds;
- 9:16;
- faceless;
- modern technology/productivity visual style;
- original narration;
- readable subtitles.

## Initial publication

YouTube Shorts first.
Publication initially manual.
Domain model must support additional platform accounts quickly.

## Forbidden/avoid initially

- medical claims;
- investment/trading promises;
- hot politics;
- celebrity gossip;
- untransformed third-party clips;
- exaggerated job-loss certainty;
- deceptive AI claims.
