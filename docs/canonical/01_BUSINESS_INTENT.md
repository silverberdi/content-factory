# Business Intent

Build a private editorial operating platform that produces and publishes original short-form content across multiple niches and languages, with progressive AI automation, human editorial control, traceability, and measurable economics.

## Economic intent

Treat channels as a portfolio of digital assets rather than an immediate primary income source.

Early north-star:
- publish acceptable-quality content repeatedly;
- learn what works by channel.

Mature north-star:
- monthly revenue per active channel versus operating cost and human effort.

Initial milestones:
- first original published short;
- repeatable 5-video/week flow;
- two comparable channels in Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese;
- USD 50/month attributable revenue;
- later USD 300–400/month;
- long-term target around USD 1,000/month from 3–5 winning channels.

## Editorial arbitrage hypothesis

Detect useful ideas early in one language/market and create genuinely original adaptations for another market where saturation may be lower.

## Explicit non-goals

- not a commercial SaaS;
- not a repost/clip farm;
- not blind autonomous publishing;
- not dozens of accounts before validation;
- not coupled to the owner's professional personal brand;
- not a months-long platform build before useful content can flow.
